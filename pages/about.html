Our Story

From a small kitchen in Riyadh to serving pizza lovers across the Kingdom.

Home › About

Pizza Kingdom was born from a simple vision: bring the world's favorite food to Saudi Arabia with an authentic Italian heart and a proudly Saudi soul. We use only the finest imported ingredients — San Marzano tomatoes, real buffalo mozzarella, cold-pressed olive oil — and combine them with local Saudi flavors like lamb kebab, Jareesh, and Mandala spices. Every pizza is halal-certified, hand-stretched, and baked in our traditional stone ovens.

Our Journey

From 2020 to Today

2020

Pizza Kingdom opens its first location in Riyadh's Olaya district. Two pizzas on the menu — and a dream.

2022

Expansion to Jeddah and Dammam. Introduction of the Saudi Special and Jareesh Pizza lines. Featured on local food blogs.

2024

12 locations across KSA. Awarded 'Best Pizza Chain' by Saudi Food Awards. Online ordering launched.

2026

50+ pizza varieties. 5,000+ happy customers daily. New branches planned in Madinah, Makkah, and Khobar.
